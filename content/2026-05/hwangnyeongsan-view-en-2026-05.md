Ask anyone in Busan about the best night view, and they'll say Gwangan Bridge. The truth is the bridge is *one piece* of the night view. The place that holds the **entire city** in a single frame is **Hwangnyeongsan Lookout**.

![Hwangnyeongsan Lookout — all of Busan in one frame](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191229151710512)
*Busan night view from Hwangnyeongsan*

## Bridge + harbor + the whole skyline

Hwangnyeongsan rises 427 m in the middle of Busan, so from the summit you see **Gwangan Bridge, Busan Port, Haeundae, Marine City, Gwangalli, Seomyeon — basically the entire city** in a 360° sweep. Once you see it, you understand the bridge is just one slice of a much bigger picture.

If you stay through golden hour → blue hour → full night, the view from any Gwangalli cafe will feel like a postcard afterward.

## Getting up — yeah, it's hard without a car

There's no nearby subway. Buses can't make the narrow road to the summit. Options:

1. **Taxi / KakaoT** — about 15,000 won (~$11) from Seomyeon, 20 min
2. **Rental car** — free summit parking, about 100 spots
3. **Taxi up + hike down** — needs proper shoes, ~1.5 hours, worth it if you like exercise

**Buses do not reach the lookout.** A lot of online info is wrong. Buses go to the trailhead at the base, but the summit is a 1-hour hike from there.

## When to arrive

Summer sunset is 7:30 PM, winter 5:30 PM. **Show up an hour before sunset.** Reasons:

1. Parking (100 spots fill up around dusk)
2. Golden hour (city in gold)
3. Blue hour (sky deep blue — best photo light)
4. Full night view (after all the lights come on)

Plan to stay 2–3 hours.

## Bring with you

- Warm layer (summit is ~5°C cooler than downtown)
- Camera + tripod (night photos *need* a tripod)
- Flashlight (dim summit lighting)
- Snacks / drinks (nearest convenience store is a 30-min walk)

## Honest take

Anyone who's only seen Gwangan Bridge hasn't actually seen *Busan's* night view. Hwangnyeongsan ruins you for any other lookout — the Gwangalli cafes feel small once you've stood up there. If you have a car, do it. If you don't, save it for your last free evening and splurge on the taxi. Best night view in the whole city. I'll fight anyone on this.
