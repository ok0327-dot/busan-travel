Every visitor to Songdo asks the same thing: "Is the cable car worth the price? Or can I just do the free Skywalk?" Done both. Short answer: do both, but **only ride the cable car once**.

![Songdo Marine Cable Car](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20200717114921294)
*Songdo cable car — 9 minutes over open water*

## Songdo Marine Cable Car — 17,000 won one-way (24,000 round-trip)

Goes from Songrim Park to Amnam Park, 1.6 km, about 9 minutes one-way over open water. Optional **Crystal Cabin** (glass floor, you see the sea under your feet) is +5,000 won.

**Pros**: Panoramic view from Haeundae all the way to Namhae. You can spot Yeongdo's cliffs in the distance. This isn't seeing Busan from below — this is Busan *from above*.
**Cons**: 17,000 won for 9 minutes is steep. Weekend lines hit 60+ minutes. Crystal Cabin lines are even worse.

## Songdo Skywalk — Free, 365 m

A reinforced-glass deck attached to the cliff at the eastern end of Songdo Beach. Stretches 365 m out over the sea. Five minutes to walk to the end. You can see the waves crashing under your feet.

**Pros**: Free. The vibration of waves underfoot is intense — way more visceral than a cable car.
**Cons**: Short. On weekend afternoons you walk slowly behind crowds. Some people freeze halfway and can't continue.

## Best route if you want both

1. **10 AM cable car** (smaller crowds) — Songrim → Amnam, *one-way only*. Saves you 8,000 won.
2. **Walk down through Amnam Park (30 min)** — surprise forest trail with a lighthouse and beach.
3. **Lunch on Songdo Beach Hoe Street** — sashimi plate runs about 50,000 won.
4. **Afternoon Skywalk** — free, take it slow even with the crowd.

![Songdo Beach](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191225175459392)
*Songdo Beach east end, where the Skywalk begins*

## Honest take

The cable car is unforgettable. But you don't ride it twice. If you were going to buy round-trip, just buy one-way and walk down through Amnam Park instead — you save 8,000 won and get a forest bonus. The Skywalk is short but free, no excuse to skip it. Same Songdo, two completely different ways to feel it: *Busan from above* vs. *Busan beneath your feet*.
