Haedong Yonggungsa is the **temple on the sea**. Korea has thousands of temples, but this is the only one bolted to a cliff with waves crashing under it. At sunrise, as the sun lifts out of the East Sea, the entire temple turns gold. The catch: most visitors **leave too late** and miss the moment.

## Timing is everything

Summer sunrise is around **5:20 AM**. Winter, around **7:20 AM**. Add 30 minutes for arrival, finding a spot, setting up your camera, and you need to be at the temple gate **30 minutes before sunrise**.

**From Haeundae**:
- Summer: leave by 4:00 AM (~30–40 min taxi + 10 min walk)
- Winter: leave by 6:00 AM

The subway's first train is 5:20 AM — too late. **Taxi or KakaoT** is the only option. Sounds hard at that hour, but Busan has 24/7 call taxis. KakaoT runs Haeundae → Yonggungsa for about 18,000 won (~$13).

## Where to stand at the temple

Entrance → 108-step staircase → main hall → **the deck next to the Sea Goddess statue**. That's where the first rays of sunlight hit. Everyone in the know stands there.

The 108 steps are narrow and dark before sunrise — bring a phone flashlight. The temple itself has minimal lighting.

## After sunrise

Sun is up by 7 AM. Don't head back yet. My recommended add-on:

1. **Temple cafe** (next to the entrance) — hot coffee while watching the morning sea
2. **Gijang Market** (10-min taxi) — anchovy or flatfish sashimi for breakfast (the local move)
3. **Back to the city** — by 9 AM, before traffic

## Honest take

Yonggungsa **during the day** is just a temple by the sea. Yonggungsa **at sunrise** is the sun rising out of the ocean, and a temple turning gold for two minutes. Anyone who's been at sunrise tells the same story — that morning was the best 4 AM of their Busan trip. Lose one night of sleep. Block out one dawn on your itinerary. That's all.
