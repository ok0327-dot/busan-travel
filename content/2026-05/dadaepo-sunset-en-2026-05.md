Where's Busan's best sunset? Most lists say Haeundae's Dongbaekseom. They're wrong. The real answer is **Dadaepo Beach** — the spot Busan locals secretly take their out-of-town guests when they want to brag.

![Dadaepo Beach sunset](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191229143230630)
*Dadaepo — Busan's widest sand stretch*

## The sand is the whole point

Dadaepo has the **widest, softest sand** in the city. You sink to your knees in it. Adults take their shoes off and wade. When the sun sets, the wet sand mirrors the sky — you see the **sunset twice**: once in the sky, once at your feet.

Haeundae's sand is too packed to reflect. Same with Gwangalli. That's why the locals migrate to Dadaepo in sunset season.

## Arrive an hour before sunset

Summer sunset is around 7:30 PM, winter around 5:30 PM. **Show up an hour before.** Three reasons:

1. Get a good spot before the crowd settles in
2. Watch the full color sequence (pink → orange → deep orange → crimson → violet)
3. Don't miss the **30-minute blue hour** right after the sun goes down

## Getting there

Subway Line 1, **Dadaepo Beach Station**, Exit 2, 5-minute walk. Free, open 24 hours.

If you drive, park near **Sunset Garden** at the north end of the beach. There's a small hill there with the best vantage — the whole beach plus Mol-undae Cliff in one frame.

## Phone photo tips

Phone cameras over-saturate sunsets every time. To keep the actual color:

- Disable auto-exposure; manually drop **-1 EV**
- Turn off any color filters
- Shoot during the **5 minutes right after the sun dips** (before blue hour starts)

## Honest take

Dadaepo is at the end of Subway Line 1 — it's a haul. Foreign tourists usually skip it. Locals know. **Don't bother on your first visit to Busan**, but if you're coming back? Block out an evening for Dadaepo. Haeundae sunsets are pretty. Dadaepo sunsets are overwhelming. Take your shoes off when you get there. Walk with the wet sand between your toes. *That's* what Busan is.
