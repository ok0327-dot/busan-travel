Want a single Busan day that *isn't* Haeundae and Gwangalli? Spend it on **Yeongdo Island**. Cliffs, cafes, lighthouse, sea wind — it's the whole island in one loop. Here's the order I send my visiting friends.

![Huinnyeoul Culture Village](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191222164810529)
*Huinnyeoul — tiny houses stacked on a Yeongdo cliff*

## 9:30 — Huinnyeoul Culture Village

From Subway Line 1 *Nampo Station*, Exit 6, take bus 7 or 7-1 (~25 min) to **Huinnyeoul Culture Village**.

The village is roughly a 1 km cliffside alley with tiny houses stacked on the slope. You'll see signs for *The Attorney* (a famous Korean movie filmed here). The real reason to come is the **Jeolyeong Coastal Walk** — a wooden boardwalk along the cliffs at the end of the village, looking straight across to Songdo.

Take an hour. Best photo spot: first deck section past the village exit.

## 11:30 — Yeongdo Cafe Strip

The end of Huinnyeoul flows directly into a cluster of cafes. *Momos Coffee Yeongdo* is the famous one (great coffee, big crowds). Most places are small terraces facing Songdo across the water. Menus are similar — pick by vibe.

Pricing: Americano 5,500–7,000 won ($4–5). Pastry + drink combo around 12,000 won ($9).

## 13:30 — Move to Taejongdae

From the Huinnyeoul stop, bus 8 or 88 (~30 min, 1,500 won) to **Taejongdae**. A taxi runs about 12,000 won.

## 14:00–16:30 — Taejongdae

(I cover the train-vs-walk debate in a separate post.) Quick version: weekday afternoons, walk to the lighthouse and take the train **one stop** back. Sinseon Rock, lighthouse cliff, sea views — 2.5 hours covers the highlights with breaks.

## 17:00 — Return

From Taejongdae stop, bus 8 → *Nampo Station* or *Busan Station*. About 30 min.

## Tips that actually save you money

- **Daily transit cost**: under 10,000 won (~$7). Buses do almost everything.
- **Shoes**: sneakers are non-negotiable for Taejongdae. Huinnyeoul boardwalk gets slippery too.
- **Timing**: leave by 9 AM if you want to skip Taejongdae train queues.
- **Lunch**: brunch at a Huinnyeoul cafe, late lunch at the *abalone porridge* shops near Taejongdae entrance.

![Taejongdae](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191222180829962)
*Taejongdae — climax of the Yeongdo loop*

## Honest take

A Yeongdo day is the best-value introduction to Busan I can think of. Not as packed as Haeundae, more substance than Gwangalli's nightlife. Cliffs + cafes + lighthouse — three different faces of the city in one walk. Next time you're in Busan, block out a whole day for Yeongdo. You won't regret it.
