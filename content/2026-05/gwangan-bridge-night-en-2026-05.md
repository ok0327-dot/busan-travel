Every visitor I host asks the same question on the first night: "Where's the best spot to see Gwangan Bridge?"

There's no single answer. I've sat at all three of the popular viewpoints and the right one depends on what you actually came for.

![Gwangalli Beach — the bridge head-on](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191229160529389)
*Right in the middle of Gwangalli — the bridge looks flat from here*

## 1. Gwangalli Beach (the obvious one)

Right on the sand, looking straight at the bridge. The vibe is unbeatable — buskers, beer, couples, families. But here's the thing: the bridge looks *flat* from this angle. You're staring at it head-on, so the curve gets lost. Photos come out looking like a postcard someone scanned.

The exception: the **Busan Fireworks Festival** every November. The fireworks burst over the bridge from the water side, and you absolutely want to be on the beach for that. Show up by 2 PM if you want a decent spot.

## 2. Marine City — the Bay 101 Walkway

This is where Instagram lives. Walk along the promenade behind The Bay 101 and the bridge curves into a perfect arc, with the skyscrapers stacked behind you and reflections doubling everything in the water.

The catch: weekend evenings after 7 PM are *brutal*. Tripod-bumping, tour-group level crowded. Go on a Tuesday around 8 PM, or hit it before sunrise on weekends. Then it's all yours.

## 3. Millak Waterfront Park — the local move

This is what Busan tastes like, honestly. You walk into Millak Hoe Center (a sashimi market right on the water), buy a *hoe* (sliced raw fish) plate, then carry it 100 meters to Millak Park, spread it out on a mat, and eat it while staring at Gwangan Bridge.

A 1.5 kg plate runs about 70,000 won (~$50). Add soju. Add the bridge lit up at 9 PM. That's a Busan night.

![Gwangan Bridge from Millak Park](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191225174730014)
*Millak Park — sashimi mat with the bridge as the backdrop*

## TL;DR

- **Best photo**: Marine City / Bay 101, weekday evening
- **Best vibe**: Gwangalli Beach
- **Most "Busan" experience**: Millak Park + sashimi
