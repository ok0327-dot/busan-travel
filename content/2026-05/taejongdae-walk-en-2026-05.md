Anyone who's been to Taejongdae splits into two camps. "The train is so convenient!" vs. "Walking is the only real way." Both are right, depending on what you came for. Let me break it down.

![Taejongdae cliffs](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191222180829962)
*The cliffs near Taejongdae lighthouse — only the unpaved trails get you here*

## The Danubi Train — 4,500 won, 4 stops

Buy tickets at the entrance. The full loop is about 30 minutes. It stops at 4 places: **Observatory, Lighthouse, Sinseon Rock, and Taejongsa Temple**. You hop off at each, look around, then catch the next train (every 5–10 minutes).

**Pros**: Easy in heat, rain, or with elderly relatives or kids. Hits the four best spots in 30 minutes. The lighthouse cliff view is genuinely jaw-dropping.
**Cons**: No freedom between stops. Lines can hit 30+ minutes on weekend afternoons (1–3 PM is the worst).

## Walking — 1.5 hours, 8.7 km loop

Take the main road from the entrance to the lighthouse. The real magic is in the **unpaved side trails** near the lighthouse — the ones the train doesn't pass. Paths through tall grass, cliff edges, sea wind, the sound of waves hitting the rocks. *That's* Yeongdo.

**Pros**: Freedom, exercise, dozens of photo spots, actual nature.
**Cons**: 1.5–2 hours (longer if you stop for photos). Sweaty in summer, slippery in rain. Honestly not recommended with elderly or small children.

## My recommended hybrid

On a non-rainy weekday morning: **walk**. But here's the trick — walk to the lighthouse, then take the train *one stop* (to Taejongsa) back toward the entrance. You skip the boring uphill return. Best of both worlds.

In rain, with parents, or with kids: **train**. Be ready to wait at the entrance gravel yard 30 minutes if the line is long.

![Huinnyeoul cliff trail](https://www.visitbusan.net/uploadImgs/files/cntnts/20191222164810529)
*Huinnyeoul (same Yeongdo district) — pairs well with Taejongdae*

## Honest take

If you take only the train at Taejongdae, you miss the cliffs of Yeongdo. The unpaved trails near the lighthouse are about 1 km from the entrance. Anyone who's stood there with the sea wind in their face actually means it when they say "Taejongdae is amazing." Wear comfortable shoes and walk at least once.
